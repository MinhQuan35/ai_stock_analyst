# What is RSI?

The Relative Strength Index (RSI) is a momentum indicator used in technical analysis.

RSI = 100 - [100 / (1 + RS)]
RS = Average Gain / Average Loss (over 14 days)

RSI values:
- Above 70: Overbought (stock may decline)
- Below 30: Oversold (stock may rise)
- Between 40-60: Neutral

RSI is calculated from price changes, not absolute prices. A 14-day period is most common.

When RSI is above 70, it means the stock has been rising strongly and may be due for a pullback.
