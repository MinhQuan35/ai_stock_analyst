# What is P/E Ratio?

The Price-to-Earnings (P/E) ratio is calculated by dividing a company's stock price by its earnings per share (EPS).

P/E = Stock Price / EPS

For example, if a stock is priced at 75,000 VND and has EPS of 4,200 VND, the P/E ratio is 17.9x.

A lower P/E ratio may indicate that a stock is undervalued. A higher P/E may suggest overvaluation or strong growth expectations.

VN stocks typically have P/E between 10-15x for value stocks and 20-30x for growth stocks.
