# Investment Strategies

## 1. Dividend Investing
- Goal: Passive income from dividends
- Suitable for: Retirees, stable income seekers
- Stocks: VCB, GAS, VHM, HPG
- Criteria: Dividend yield > 3%, payout ratio < 60%

## 2. Growth Investing
- Goal: Long-term capital appreciation
- Suitable for: Under 35, risk-tolerant investors
- Stocks: FPT, VIC, MSN
- Criteria: Revenue growth > 20%, ROE > 15%

## 3. Value Investing
- Goal: Buy undervalued stocks
- Suitable for: Patient, analytical investors
- Stocks: P/E < 10, P/B < 1
- Example: MBB, CTG, HPG

## 4. Index Investing
- Goal: Track VN30, S&P500
- Suitable for: Those who don't want to pick stocks
- Products: ETF VFMVN30, ETF SSI-SCA
- Benefits: Diversification, low fees

## 5. Swing Trading
- Goal: Short-term gains (1-4 weeks)
- Suitable for: Active traders
- Tools: MA, RSI, MACD
- Risk: High, requires discipline
