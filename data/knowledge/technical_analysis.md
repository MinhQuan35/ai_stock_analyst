# Stock Technical Analysis

## 1. Moving Averages (MA)

### SMA (Simple Moving Average)
- MA5: Short-term trend (5 days)
- MA20: Short-term trend (20 days)
- MA50: Medium-term trend (50 days)
- MA200: Long-term trend (200 days)

### Signals
- Price crosses above MA → Buy signal (Golden Cross)
- Price crosses below MA → Sell signal (Death Cross)
- MA200 is strong support level

## 2. RSI (Relative Strength Index)

### Formula
RSI = 100 - [100 / (1 + RS)]
RS = Average Gain / Average Loss (14 days)

### Meaning
- RSI > 70: Overbought → May decline
- RSI < 30: Oversold → May rise
- RSI 40-60: Neutral

## 3. MACD (Moving Average Convergence Divergence)

### Formula
- MACD Line = EMA12 - EMA26
- Signal Line = EMA9 of MACD Line
- Histogram = MACD Line - Signal Line

### Signals
- MACD crosses above Signal → Buy
- MACD crosses below Signal → Sell
- Histogram increasing → Bullish momentum
- Histogram decreasing → Bearish momentum

## 4. Volume Analysis

### Meaning
- Volume increase + Price increase → Strong trend
- Volume decrease + Price increase → Weak trend
- Volume spike → News event or institutional activity
