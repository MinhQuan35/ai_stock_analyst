# Stock Fundamental Analysis

## 1. Key Valuation Metrics

### P/E Ratio (Price-to-Earnings)
- Formula: Stock Price / Earnings Per Share (EPS)
- Meaning: Years to recover investment if earnings stay constant
- VN Benchmark: 10-15x (low = cheap), >20x (high = expensive)
- Example: VNM P/E = 18 → More expensive than industry average

### P/B Ratio (Price-to-Book)
- Formula: Stock Price / Book Value Per Share
- Meaning: Compare market price to net asset value
- P/B < 1: Stock may be undervalued
- P/B > 3: High valuation

### Dividend Yield
- Formula: (Annual Dividend / Stock Price) × 100%
- Meaning: Return from dividends
- Above 5%: High dividend (check sustainability)

## 2. Real Examples

### VNM (Vinamilk)
- Price: 75,000 VND
- EPS: 4,200 VND
- P/E = 75,000 / 4,200 = 17.8x
- Dividend: 3,000 VND → Yield = 4%

### VIC (Vingroup)
- Price: 45,000 VND
- EPS: 1,500 VND
